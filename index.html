About

Analytical work, built for scrutiny.

My background is in forensic and investigative data analytics: building the ETL pipelines, statistical models, and evidentiary datasets that support legal counsel, regulators, and expert witnesses in high-stakes financial investigations. That work has a way of making you precise: when a number ends up in a regulatory filing or a courtroom exhibit, it has to be right the first time.

I bring that same rigor to broader analytics and data engineering work: designing data models, automating reconciliation and reporting, and translating messy, multi-source data into something decision-makers can actually use. I care as much about the pipeline being reproducible as I do about the answer being correct.

Working as a technical consultant has also taught me that I won't always start a project with the domain expertise, industry background, or even technical toolkit it demands, and that's never been a dealbreaker. I have a fast learning curve, I ask good questions, and I approach unfamiliar territory with a teachable, willing spirit: whether that means leveraging AI for deeper research, digging in on my own, or simply asking the right people the right questions. I've learned I can teach myself almost anything a project needs.

Outside of client work, I spend most of my time outdoors. See Beyond the Desk below.
