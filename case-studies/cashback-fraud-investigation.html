Credit Card Cashback Offer Fraud Investigation

PII fuzzy matching and behavioral analysis to uncover systematic misuse of a credit card cashback offer program.

PythonSQLFuzzy MatchingPII MatchingBehavioral Analytics

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A credit card cashback offer program showed signs of systematic misuse, requiring an investigation into coordinated multi-card enrollment and manipulation of third-party invoice payments to extract rewards.

The Approach

  • Analyzed credit card enrollment and transaction data to identify cardholders who enrolled multiple cards in the cashback offer program, receiving disproportionate rewards, in some cases upwards of $1,600 per individual.
  • Reconstructed the fraud pattern involving overpayment of third-party invoices via credit card followed by refund requests, enabling cardholders to retain cashback rewards while recovering the underlying payment.
  • Implemented fuzzy matching logic using Python's FuzzyWuzzy package to perform PII matching between cardholder records and business addresses, surfacing linked individuals and entities obscured by name variations, address formatting differences, and data entry inconsistencies.
Fuzzy PII matching linked cardholders to third-party businesses that exact-match methods never would have caught.

The Impact

  • Identified a population of cardholders systematically exploiting the cashback offer structure through multi-card enrollment and invoice overpayment schemes.
  • PII fuzzy matching logic surfaced linkages between cardholders and third-party businesses that would have been undetectable through exact-match methods, expanding the identified fraud population.
  • Delivered structured analytical findings providing investigators with a clear, evidence-based picture of the fraud mechanism and affected population.
