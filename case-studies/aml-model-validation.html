AML Model Validation (SR 11-7)

An independent model validation of customer risk-rating and transaction-monitoring models for a global cryptocurrency exchange, spanning multiple datacenters and jurisdictions.

Model ValidationAMLSQL PythonSR 11-7FATF / FFIEC

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A global cryptocurrency exchange needed an independent model validation, covering both its customer risk-rating model and its transaction-monitoring model, across multiple datacenters and jurisdictions, in accordance with SR 11-7 model risk management standards. The goal was an audit-ready assessment the exchange could stand behind under regulatory scrutiny.

The Approach

  • Led end-to-end validation of a customer risk rating model assessing behavioral and identity risk signals, including source of funds, source of wealth, purpose of account, cross-border transaction volume, high-risk counterparty concentration, shared device indicators, VPN/TOR network usage, and product usage patterns.
  • Validated transaction monitoring rules on a rule-by-rule basis, including structured fiat deposit sequences followed by crypto transfers to high-risk addresses, large cross-border transactions originating from high-risk jurisdictions, and activity patterns inconsistent with stated customer profiles.
  • Reviewed alignment between model documentation and underlying code logic, identifying gaps where implemented methodology deviated from documented design, producing a formal inventory of findings with severity classifications and remediation recommendations.
  • Assessed the exchange's risk appetite framework and evaluated whether monitoring coverage adequately addressed risks introduced by new product releases and emerging fraud typologies.
  • Produced a comprehensive model validation report organizing findings across conceptual soundness, data integrity, model performance, and documentation adequacy workstreams.
An audit-ready, SR 11-7-aligned validation spanning two model types and multiple jurisdictions.

The Impact

  • Delivered a formal model validation report across two model types, customer risk rating and transaction monitoring, covering multiple datacenters and jurisdictions, providing the client with an audit-ready independent assessment.
  • Identified documentation-to-code misalignments and feature design deficiencies across key risk rating components, enabling the client to prioritize remediation efforts ahead of regulatory review.
  • Surfaced monitoring gaps tied to emerging product risk and new fraud patterns, providing actionable recommendations to strengthen the client's BSA/AML program in alignment with FATF guidance and FFIEC examination standards.
