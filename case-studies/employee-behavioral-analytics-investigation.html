Employee Behavioral Analytics & Fraud Investigation

A consolidated behavioral analytics pipeline supporting an internal investigation into potential fraud and policy violations across 300+ employees.

DatabricksPythonSQLETLBehavioral Analytics

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

Outside legal counsel needed support investigating potential fraud and policy violations across a population of 300+ employees at a major financial services corporation, requiring the consolidation of behavioral, financial, and operational data to identify anomalous conduct.

The Approach

  • Developed an automated Databricks ingestion pipeline to consolidate internal company data across a wide range of sources, including credit card charges and payments, personal loans, high-yield savings accounts, investment accounts, peer-to-peer transfers, HR and compliance records, and office badge swipe data.
  • Engineered individual employee-level work product providing a consolidated behavioral profile for each subject, surfacing anomalous transaction patterns, policy violations, and indicators of potential fraud across all data sources.
  • Assisted legal counsel in developing targeted interview questions informed by observed unusual transaction behavior, supporting the investigative and interview preparation process for employees under review.
Consolidated behavioral profiles across 300+ employees and eight-plus data sources into one investigative view per subject.

The Impact

  • Delivered consolidated behavioral profiles across 300+ employees, enabling counsel to systematically prioritize individuals of investigative interest across a large, complex subject population.
  • Surfaced anomalous patterns across credit card activity, inter-account transfers, and access records, providing counsel with a structured, multi-dimensional evidentiary foundation for employee interviews.
  • Accelerated the investigative timeline through automation of data ingestion and profile generation across disparate internal data systems.
