Consumer Finance Compliance Gap Assessment

An independent gap assessment and remediation roadmap for a community bank operating under two active FDIC Consent Orders.

IT GovernanceCode ReviewVulnerability AssessmentRegulatory Compliance

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A community bank operating under two active FDIC Consent Orders needed an independent analyst to validate remediation progress, identify outstanding gaps, and build a structured roadmap for corrective action completion across safety, soundness, and compliance risk domains.

The Approach

  • Conducted a comprehensive gap assessment against FDIC Consent Order requirements, collecting and validating corrective action evidence through stakeholder interviews, documentation review, and progress report analysis.
  • Evaluated remediation completeness across policies and procedures, compliance management systems, board and management oversight structures, risk assessments, and committee charters, flagging outstanding deficiencies and prioritizing by regulatory risk.
  • Assessed asset and liability management policies, loans to insiders frameworks, affiliate policies, code of ethics enhancements, and audit and internal controls for adequacy and regulatory alignment.
  • Assisted in root cause analysis for unresolved findings and supported development of corrective action programs, including reporting frameworks, compliance management system builds, vendor management programs, and monitoring and testing structures.
  • Produced a formal gap assessment report documenting remediation status, remaining deficiencies, ownership assignments, and a sequenced action plan for regulatory close-out.
A sequenced remediation roadmap helped a community bank prioritize its highest-risk findings under two concurrent FDIC Consent Orders.

The Impact

  • Delivered a structured gap assessment and remediation roadmap enabling the bank to prioritize its highest-risk outstanding findings under two concurrent FDIC Consent Orders.
  • Identified gaps in compliance management infrastructure, including monitoring programs, committee governance, and vendor oversight, that had not been fully resolved through prior remediation efforts.
  • Provided the institutional framework for sustainable regulatory compliance, reducing the bank's exposure to unresolved consent order findings and supporting a credible response to ongoing FDIC oversight.
