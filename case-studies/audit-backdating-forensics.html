Audit Backdating Metadata Forensics

Forensic metadata analysis supporting an internal investigation into potential audit backdating by partners at a professional services firm.

DatabricksPythonSQLMetadata AnalysisForensic Analytics

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

Outside legal counsel needed forensic support for an internal investigation into potential audit backdating by partners at a professional services firm, requiring metadata analysis and discrepancy identification to support interview preparation and investigative findings, despite significant data quality gaps.

The Approach

  • Developed a Databricks-based ETL script to ingest, load, and standardize DBF files containing audit signoff metadata, consolidating disparate data sources into a unified analytical dataset.
  • Performed comparative analysis between extracted metadata timestamps and front-end GUI audit records to identify discrepancies indicative of backdated partner signoffs.
  • Worked through significant data quality gaps, including wiped or overwritten metadata, to identify and implement analytical workarounds that preserved the integrity of the backdating analysis.
  • Prepared multiple structured reports for counsel summarizing discrepancy findings, supporting investigation strategy and interview preparation for partners identified as at risk.
  • Collaborated closely with legal counsel throughout the engagement to refine analytical direction and ensure findings were framed to address the specific needs of the investigation.
Recovered defensible forensic signal from wiped audit metadata, preserving evidentiary value under adverse data conditions.

The Impact

  • Surfaced timestamp discrepancies between metadata extracts and GUI records, providing counsel with forensic evidence of potential backdating across the partner population under review.
  • Developed defensible workarounds to recover analytical signal despite wiped metadata, preserving the evidentiary value of the dataset under adverse data conditions.
  • Delivered structured, counsel-ready reports enabling targeted interview preparation for at-risk partners.
