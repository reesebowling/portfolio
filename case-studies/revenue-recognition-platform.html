Enterprise Revenue Recognition Platform

A production-deployed revenue recognition and forecasting model built to unify billions of transaction records across five-plus disconnected systems.

RedshiftSQLPython ETLTableauProduction Deployment

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A private-equity-backed healthcare company needed a single, reliable revenue recognition and forecasting solution: one capable of integrating billions of historical claim and payment transaction records scattered across five-plus disconnected systems into one production-grade reporting platform finance leadership could trust for month-end close.

The Approach

  • Developed a revenue recognition data model in Redshift integrating billions of historical claim and payment transaction records aggregated from 5+ disparate source systems, including NetSuite extracts, backend Redshift data tables, and manually generated datasets.
  • Built an automated ETL pipeline to standardize, reconcile, and load multi-source financial data into the revenue model, and assisted with end-to-end deployment of the solution to the client's production environment.
  • Designed a Tableau dashboard surfacing forward-looking payment forecasts and cash flow projections, leveraging historical payment cycle behavior across multi-year datasets to support executive financial planning and reporting.
Billions of transaction records. Five-plus source systems. One production-deployed model finance leadership relies on for month-end close.

The Impact

  • Delivered a production-deployed revenue recognition model consolidating 5+ financial systems and billions of transaction records, providing the client with a reliable, scalable foundation for month-end close and financial reporting.
  • Enabled forward-looking cash flow visibility through Tableau forecasting dashboards, equipping finance leadership with data-driven projections grounded in multi-year historical payment patterns.
  • Automated previously manual reconciliation and reporting workflows, materially reducing processing time and improving analytical accuracy across the revenue recognition process.
