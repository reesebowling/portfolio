Fair Lending Statistical Review

Third-party independent statistical analysis supporting a fair lending investigation for a financial services provider operating under an FDIC Consent Order.

AWS SageMakerSQLPython DuckDBRegressionChi-Squared / Z-Tests

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

Embedded on a small analytics team, I served as a key technical resource in a fair lending investigation for a major financial services provider operating under an FDIC Consent Order. The task: independently and defensibly evaluate lending outcomes across 120 credit products for evidence of disparate treatment, at a scale of 150 million-plus applications.

The Approach

  • Engineered reproducible ETL workflows to ingest, normalize, and reconcile 150M+ credit applications across disparate internal sources into a structured warehouse environment purpose-built for regulatory analysis.
  • Designed analytical data models spanning 120 credit products with 30+ underwriting attributes per product, incorporating demographic segmentation, approval/denial outcomes, and model feature attribution outputs.
  • Built automated statistical testing pipelines in Python (NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn) executing chi-squared tests, linear and logistic regression, and data validation routines across large, high-variance datasets.
  • Translated statistical outputs into evidence-based findings for dual audiences: regulatory examiners (FDIC) and internal client stakeholders, supporting compliance remediation under a formal consent order.
150M+ credit applications reconciled and modeled across 120 credit products to support a formal regulatory response.

The Impact

  • Supported identification of potential fair lending violations across a portfolio of 120 credit products serving millions of applicants.
  • Processed and reconciled 150M+ credit application records spanning 2021–2022, enabling comprehensive demographic and underwriting analysis at institutional scale.
  • Delivered analytical outputs directly informing the client's regulatory response to the FDIC, with findings used as evidentiary support in a federal consent order proceeding.
