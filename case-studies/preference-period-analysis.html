Preference Period & Asset Recovery Analysis

A multi-stage population-selection framework and dual-valuation recovery model built to support a post-bankruptcy preference period investigation for a collapsed cryptocurrency exchange.

PythonSQLExcel Modeling Financial RemediationPII Clustering

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

Following the collapse of a cryptocurrency trading platform, legal counsel needed to identify which account holders had engaged in suspicious activity ahead of the bankruptcy filing, and to quantify potential recovery amounts — starting from the platform's entire account population and narrowing to a defensible set of investigative targets.

The Approach

  • Analyzed the full account population's transaction history — deposits, purchases, sales, and withdrawals — across the preference period to identify anomalous trading patterns and activity indicative of insider knowledge preceding the bankruptcy filing.
  • Engineered a multi-stage waterfall population-selection framework, applying layered inclusion and exclusion criteria including withdrawal thresholds ($100K+ in crypto withdrawals, or $100K+ in crypto sales with concurrent USD withdrawals), purchase-to-withdrawal ratios, portfolio value change thresholds, and null-value handling logic — ultimately scoping the eligible population to ~1,200 customers.
  • Collaborated with counsel to develop and iteratively refine preference analysis logic, incorporating attorney-directed email review findings to further narrow the targeted population to 47 accounts of investigative interest.
  • Constructed dual-methodology preference calculations — a transfer-date valuation model and a highest-asset valuation model — sourcing historical coin pricing with defensible fallback logic for coins with unavailable pricing data.
  • Performed account-level PII clustering using phone number and address field combinations to identify linked accounts and related-party relationships across the broader population.
  • Produced summary reports, descriptive statistics, and a Tableau dashboard visualizing trading trends and activity concentrations for counsel.
From an entire platform's account population to the ~50 highest-priority targets for legal action and recovery.

The Impact

  • Scoped a universe of ~1,200 preference-eligible accounts from the full platform population through a rigorous, defensible multi-criteria waterfall methodology.
  • Delivered a targeted population of ~50 high-priority accounts for legal action, directly informing counsel's preference claim filings and asset recovery proceedings.
  • Produced dual-valuation preference calculations — including a highest-asset methodology capturing peak coin values — quantifying the full range of potential recovery amounts for litigation purposes.
  • Enabled legal counsel to identify potential insider trading activity and related-party account clusters, supporting investigative findings in a high-profile platform collapse.
