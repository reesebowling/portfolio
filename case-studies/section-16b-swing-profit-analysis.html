Section 16(b) Short-Swing Profit Analysis

A securities law expert report quantifying short-swing profit liability under Section 16(b) across a major financial institution's closed-end fund trading activity.

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A securities law expert witness engaged by outside counsel needed to assess potential short-swing profit liabilities under Section 16(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 for a major financial institution's registered closed-end fund trading activity, across a highly fragmented set of internal trading systems.

The Approach

  • Consolidated and harmonized trading data across five internal bank platforms, spanning equities, warrants, rights, fixed income, listed options, OTC options, and swaps, identified by CUSIP and internal security identifiers, into a unified analytical dataset.
  • Built automated exploratory data analysis profiling scripts to systematically surface data quality issues, flag inconsistencies across platforms, and validate completeness of position and transaction records prior to expert analysis.
  • Reconciled end-of-day position data against transaction-level records across core trading systems to confirm data integrity and ensure no reportable activity was omitted from the liability analysis.
  • Applied a 10% beneficial ownership threshold across the full closed-end fund population to identify funds where the bank's aggregate holdings triggered Section 16(b) reporting obligations, accounting for complex instrument types including derivatives and OTC products.
  • Constructed matched purchase-and-sale schedules across a six-month short-swing window for each triggered fund, calculating disgorgeable profit amounts per the statutory formula and applicable SEC guidance.
  • Prepared structured analytical workproduct organized by fund, transaction pair, and profit amount, suitable for regulatory submission and potential litigation use.
Reconciled trading data across five internal platforms to scope Section 16(b) exposure fund by fund, transaction pair by transaction pair.

The Impact

  • Processed and reconciled trading activity across five disparate internal platforms encompassing equity, fixed income, listed options, and OTC derivative instruments, establishing a defensible, audit-ready dataset for expert testimony.
  • Identified the subset of positions breaching the 10% beneficial ownership threshold, enabling precise scoping of Section 16(b) liability exposure across a complex, multi-instrument trading book.
  • Delivered structured short-swing profit calculations and supporting workpapers that directly informed the expert's report to outside counsel, providing a quantified liability estimate for regulatory engagement and potential disgorgement proceedings.
