Anti-Bribery & Corruption Model Validation

An independent model validation of an internally built bribery detection tool for the Anti-Bribery group at a large global investment bank.

PythonSQLModel ValidationStochastic OptimizationGap Analysis

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

The Anti-Bribery group and Engineering teams at a large global investment bank needed an independent model validation of an internally built bribery detection tool, assessing conceptual soundness, data integrity, and scenario performance.

The Approach

  • Reviewed policy documentation and business requirements to assess whether the bribery detection model was fit for purpose, evaluating alignment between stated design intent and implemented logic.
  • Conducted iterative scenario data review and targeted questioning to validate data elements against model documentation, identifying gaps between documented and operationalized methodology.
  • Performed scenario-level analysis to develop specific recommendations for reducing false positive volumes, assessing detection thresholds and score coefficient calibration across the bribery scenario population.
  • Deployed a differential evolution stochastic optimization algorithm across each scenario population to optimize score coefficients and detection thresholds, producing a set of calibrated parameters for improved bribery detection performance.
  • Conducted a gap analysis against applicable industry and regulatory standards to identify unaddressed risk typologies and define recommendations for new scenario development.
Stochastic optimization recalibrated detection thresholds across every bribery scenario, cutting false positives with a defensible, data-driven basis.

The Impact

  • Delivered optimized detection parameters across bribery scenarios through differential evolution modeling, providing the client with a quantified, defensible basis for threshold recalibration.
  • Identified documentation-to-implementation gaps and false positive reduction opportunities, equipping the client with actionable short and long-term recommendations to maintain market-leading and regulatory-compliant anti-bribery controls.
  • Produced a structured gap analysis against industry and regulatory standards, enabling the client to prioritize new scenario development in alignment with evolving AML and anti-bribery requirements.
