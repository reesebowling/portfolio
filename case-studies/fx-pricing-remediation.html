FX Pricing Practice Investigation & Client Remediation

Investigative analytics and client-level remediation calculations following alleged improper foreign exchange pricing practices at a multinational financial services company.

SQL (CTEs, Window Functions, Recursion)CRM Data IntegrationFinancial Remediation

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A multinational financial services company faced regulatory scrutiny and press coverage over alleged improper foreign exchange pricing practices. Outside legal counsel needed investigative analytics to identify violations, followed by a remediation calculation for affected clients.

The Approach

  • Mined and integrated data across two disparate enterprise systems, the company's full transactional data warehouse and its CRM platform, to evaluate pricing fidelity and client communication records across thousands of client relationships.
  • Built a structured keyword search tool enabling counsel to efficiently query CRM data using investigative search terms, surfacing and flagging relevant hits to streamline attorney review at scale.
  • Constructed client-level chronologies marrying transaction history, client correspondence, and rate change records, creating a unified, reviewable record of each client's pricing experience for counsel and regulators.
  • Identified instances where clients had received pricing promises that were not fulfilled, producing findings subsequently reported to US regulators.
  • Recreated thousands of individual foreign exchange transactions at the client-specific pricing rates that had been communicated but not honored, establishing the factual basis for repayment calculations.
  • Calculated remediation amounts including applicable interest for each affected client, with outputs directly driving repayment distributions.
A purpose-built CRM search tool compressed a multi-thousand-client review into a workable investigative timeline.

The Impact

  • Findings presented to US regulators formed the evidentiary basis for a formal regulatory resolution, with remediation outcomes subsequently covered by a major business publication.
  • Enabled counsel to efficiently review thousands of client records through a purpose-built CRM search tool, materially compressing the investigative timeline.
  • Delivered client-level remediation calculations at scale, resulting in confirmed repayments, including interest, to all identified affected parties.
