Treasury Trading Surveillance Model

A custom-built surveillance model to detect spoofing and layering activity across treasury and futures markets for a multi-national bank's internal investigation.

PythonSQLAWS PowerShellBCP / Command LinePlotly

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

Legal counsel needed to determine whether high-frequency traders at a multi-national bank had engaged in spoofing and layering (placing orders with no intent to execute them, in order to manipulate prices) across treasury and futures markets. These patterns are difficult to detect because doing so requires reconstructing intent from an enormous volume of order and trade data spanning a decade, sourced from disparate internal systems.

The Approach

  • Engineered a custom trading surveillance model in Python and SQL to detect spoofing and layering activity across single- and cross-market trading patterns.
  • Architected an end-to-end ETL pipeline using dynamic SQL stored procedures and BCP/command-line tooling to ingest, clean, and de-duplicate 1,000+ flat files across disparate client data sources.
  • Partnered with expert witnesses in securities law, accounting, and regulatory enforcement to translate domain knowledge into quantitative detection logic within the surveillance model.
  • Integrated Limit Order Book data to perform pricing impact analysis, quantifying the market price effect of identified spoofing activity on treasury bond valuations.
  • Produced 100+ regulatory exhibits and interactive Plotly/HTML visualizations to support legal counsel in presenting findings to investigators.
200B+ transaction records processed and synthesized to surface 500+ instances of suspected spoofing and layering across 10+ employees and a 10-year trading history.

The Impact

  • Identified 500+ instances of spoofing and layering across 10+ employees spanning a 10-year trading history.
  • Processed and synthesized 200B+ transaction records, enabling comprehensive pattern detection at institutional scale.
  • Automated ingestion and cleaning of 1,000+ flat files, eliminating manual data handling and accelerating the investigation timeline.
