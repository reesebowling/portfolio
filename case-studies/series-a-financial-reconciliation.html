Series A Financial Reconciliation for a DOJ Investigation

Financial reconciliation and data room review supporting a DOJ investigation into the Series A financing of a cryptocurrency exchange.

SQLPythonETLFinancial ReconciliationData Room Review

Note: client and institution names are withheld per confidentiality requirements. This engagement is described using generalized role and industry context only.

The Challenge

A DOJ investigation into the Series A financing transaction of a cryptocurrency exchange required evaluating the financial information provided to investors and reconciling exchange-reported business metrics against underlying transactional data.

The Approach

  • Reviewed financial documents included in investor data rooms, including audited financial statements, and evaluated the evidentiary support for key Series A financial metrics provided to investors, including transaction volume, gross revenue, and net revenue.
  • Assessed the accuracy and completeness of financial information disclosed across multiple investor-specific data rooms, identifying discrepancies between representations made to different investor groups.
  • Developed SQL and Python scripts to perform reconciliation of exchange-level transaction data against trading cost line items on the profit and loss spreadsheet, validating the integrity of reported financial metrics against underlying records.
Reconciled exchange-reported revenue against underlying transaction data, surfacing disclosure inconsistencies across investor tiers.

The Impact

  • Delivered a structured reconciliation of exchange transaction data to profit and loss trading costs, providing investigators with a quantified view of discrepancies between reported and underlying financial metrics.
  • Evaluated investor data room materials across multiple access tiers, surfacing inconsistencies in financial disclosures relevant to the DOJ investigation.
  • Produced audit-ready analytical workproduct supporting expert assessment of the Series A transaction's financial representations.
